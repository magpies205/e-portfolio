News App

For this assignment, I had the app receive user input by allowing the users to like news articles and save them. The app navigatied between two fragments of the main news page and the saved news articles page. These news articles were taken from the internet through a databse with news articles. Also, it stores the information of the user's liked articles into the device. It allows users to go to the liked news articles and swipe them to delete the articles as well.
