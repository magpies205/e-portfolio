Concert Connect

For this project, I designed a music discovery app that allows users to follow bands, save songs and concerts, and receive notifications for updates. The app allows users to search for concerts nearby and engage with their favorite bands by following them. I conducted interviews to define user needs and pain points, I created user personas and empathy maps to better understand target users and inform design decisions. I developed wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes using Figma and designed the logo in Canva.
